How to play
  • You run the offense. Your defense's drives are simulated from its rating.
  • Pass: after the snap, drag backwards anywhere on the field (like a slingshot) and release. The ring shows where the ball lands — lead your receiver.
  • Scramble: instead of throwing, swipe toward the end zone (or press right arrow / D) and your quarterback tucks it and runs.
  • Running: whoever has the ball (back, receiver after the catch, scrambling quarterback) runs straight ahead on his own. Swipe up or down to cut toward a sideline (or hold W/S / the arrow keys).
  • Juke: tap, then swipe (or press space, then up/down). Time it just before a defender reaches you and you slip the tackle. Juke too early and you stumble, and you need a moment before the next one. Your running back's Agility makes the timing more forgiving, shortens the recovery, and decides whether a good tackler reads the juke.
  • Tackling: every defender has a tackling rating (TKL). Head-on hits from good tacklers almost always land; grabs from the side or behind miss far more, especially on a fast runner, so cut away from defenders instead of into them. Runners fall forward for extra yards, and a big head-on hit can occasionally knock the ball loose.
  • Kicks: tap (or press space) when the marker is inside the green zone.
  • Win games to earn coaching points and upgrade your roster between weeks. Each player's stars set how high his stats can go; the tick on each bar is his cap.
  • QB stamina: the bar over your quarterback drains every snap. When it runs low his throws get shorter and less accurate. It refills at the start of each drive.
