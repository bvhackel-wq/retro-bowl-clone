How to play
  • You run the offense. Your defense's drives are simulated from its rating.
  • Pass: after the snap, drag backwards anywhere on the field (like a slingshot) and release. The ring shows where the ball lands — lead your receiver.
  • Run / after the catch: your ball carrier runs straight ahead on his own. Swipe up or down to cut toward a sideline (or hold W/S / the arrow keys).
  • Juke: tap, then swipe (or press space, then up/down). Time it just before a defender reaches you and you slip the tackle. Juke too early and you stumble, and you need a moment before the next one.
  • Kicks: tap (or press space) when the marker is inside the green zone.
  • Win games to earn coaching points and upgrade your roster between weeks.
